WHO chief says life expectancy has fallen from 78.8 years in 2020 to 74.4 in 2025, figures that predate US fuel blockade Life expectancy has fallen for the first time in Cuba since 1950 as the island reels from a severe economic crisis, the head of the World Health Organization (WHO) said on Tuesday.
