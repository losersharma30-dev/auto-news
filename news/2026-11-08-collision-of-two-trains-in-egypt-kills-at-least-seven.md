President orders prompt investigation after one train derails in collision in al-Ayyat about 30 miles from Cairo Seven people were killed and more than 100 injured when two trains collided on Sunday south of the Egyptian capital, Cairo, officials said. The crash occurred in al-Ayyat district in Giza.
